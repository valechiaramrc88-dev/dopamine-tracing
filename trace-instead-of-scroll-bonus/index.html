Printing tips

  • Print at 100% / "Actual size" on US Letter or A4 paper.
  • Using markers? Print on thicker paper (cardstock, 65–110 lb) and still slide a sheet of card underneath.
  • Print only the pages you want. On a phone? Open the PDF and use "Share → Print", or save it and print later from a computer.
Same rule as the book: fill the soft gray shapes with ink and keep the white gaps white. Wobbles are welcome. Put the phone down, pick a pattern, enjoy your evening.
